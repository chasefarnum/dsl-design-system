Community Information

Sunrise Gardens is an award-winning senior living community offering Independent Living, Assisted Living, and Memory Care. Nestled on beautifully landscaped grounds in Orlando, FL, our community offers resort-style amenities including a heated pool, fitness center, fine dining, and vibrant social programming.

Our dedicated care team is available 24/7 to ensure the comfort, safety, and wellbeing of every resident. We believe that every day should be filled with purpose, joy, and connection.

Monthly pricing starts at $3,200 for Independent Living and varies based on care level and apartment size. Contact us for a personalized pricing quote.
